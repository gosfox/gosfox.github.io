Projects

  • ADWUD (Aplikacja do wspomagania układania dyżyrów) - a polish windows C# WinForms .NET application created for helping with the process of assigning teacher's supervisory duties.

    Is able to export to/inport from .xlsx, export to a stylized A3 .pdf, take data on its own from the specified schedule website, save and read data in .csv, include/exclude classes or teachers, count minutes based on teacher's hours, colour cells based on availability, visualize schedules in-app and more.

    Most tools that do this do not have in mind constant class-wide internships or two buildings specification. To help our school, I built this app with the help of a classmate. It's still in development, though ready for early use and already proved to be helpful.

    Future updates include a.o. giving access to other schools, mobile app and web viewer.

  • Dollhut - a more accessible version of Touyhou.se.

    Still in very early development, but should be open for early use soon.

    Touhouse is a good website. Excellent place for artists to keep their own characters. Except, to even join, you need to aquire an invite code. And then, when you do find one, getting lost is very easy. I've heard from multiple people who give out codes say, that the people they invited looked around and left. Of course, no hate to Toyhouse, but the amount of overwhelming options makes it hard to navigate.

    To let people have a simpler place to keep their universes, I decided to make a "clone" in my style. You login via GitHub and keep your data as a repository. If anything happens to the site, your data is still safe on your account. This architecture also opens multiple paths for future growth, since the neccesary data is stored publicly. For the far future, I'm already thinking about everntually making a mobile app.
